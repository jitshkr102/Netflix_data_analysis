# Netflix_data_analysis
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Netflix Movies and TV Shows using Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn. This project explores content types, genres, countries, directors, release years, movie durations, and content additions to identify patterns and insights in Netflix's content catalogue.


## Objectives

- Analyze the distribution of Movies and TV Shows on Netflix.
- Identify popular genres and countries represented in the catalogue.
- Explore director contributions and content release-year trends.
- Identify the shortest and longest movies by duration.
- Analyze trends in the years titles were added to Netflix.
- Perform data cleaning and handle missing values.
- Use Python visualizations to discover patterns and meaningful insights from the dataset.

- ## Scope

- Analyze Netflix Movies and TV Shows using exploratory data analysis techniques.
- Examine content types, genres, countries, directors, release years, and movie durations.
- Explore trends in the years and months when titles were added to Netflix.
- Identify missing values and assess data quality before analysis.
- Use Python libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to summarize and visualize findings.
- Provide descriptive insights into Netflix's content catalogue that may support content planning and strategic discussions.

- ### Business Impact

- Helps stakeholders understand the composition of Netflix's content catalogue.
- Identifies popular genres and content-producing countries to support content strategy discussions.
- Reveals content release and addition trends that may inform future acquisition and production planning.
- Provides insights into movie durations and the balance between Movies and TV Shows.
- Demonstrates how data analytics can support informed business decisions, while recognizing that catalogue data alone cannot establish viewer preferences or business performance.

**Limitation:** This project focuses on the available dataset and does not analyze viewer preferences, streaming performance, revenue, or subscriber behavior.
